01 / Site intelligence

A wooded valley site approximately 35 minutes from a major tourism core, with seasonal stream frontage and a setting suitable for nature-oriented hospitality.

Advantages

  • Quiet nature-rich setting and mountain outlook
  • Proximity to an established tourism destination
  • Room for landscaping, spa and yoga pavilions
  • Opportunity for boutique dining and small retreats
  • Strong distinctiveness potential through architecture

Drawbacks

  • No direct river access
  • Approx. 35-minute transfer from tourist core
  • Seasonal flood, drainage and stream setbacks require checks
  • Uneven terrain may raise capex
  • Limited walkable dining and entertainment nearby

All site claims are invented. Flood, environmental, access and hotel-approval feasibility must be established with competent specialists before investment.

Concept recommendation

Position around scenery, wellbeing, signature F&B and two- or three-night escapes. Test a memorable pool, yoga deck, curated excursions, wellness rooms and one flexible smaller retreat space. Avoid committing to large conference facilities based on the comparatively limited stated preference.

ALTERNATIVE STUDY RECOMMENDED

48-key wellness concept vs 36-key lower-capex resort

08 / Next validation steps

  1. Survey consented target guests by source market, income, group composition and trip type.
  2. Test room packages using realistic property mock-ups and controlled price questions.
  3. Benchmark ADR, occupancy and reviews of a clearly defined competing hotel set.
  4. Investigate catchment access, hazards, stream setbacks and hotel licensing.
  5. Cost the 48-key and 36-key concepts and run sensitivity analyses.
  6. Review the output with an experienced hotel operator and hospitality feasibility team.
