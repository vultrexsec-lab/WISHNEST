01 / Site assessment

A wooded, sloping hillside site positioned as a gated second-home community. The site offers desirable upper-ridge views, while its lower plots and infrastructure impose design and cost constraints.

Site advantages

  • Panoramic views from upper ridge
  • Existing mature pine and oak landscape
  • Quiet, low-density setting
  • Opportunity for boutique clubhouse and wellness areas
  • Appeal to short-break second-home users

Site drawbacks

  • Views obstructed from roughly 40% of lower site
  • Tree protection constrains building footprints
  • Steep terrain and internal road engineering
  • Approx. 4 m approach road width
  • Longer journey to retail and medical services

The 24-villa yield is not approved. Access, slope stability, drainage, tree protection, fire access, legal buyer eligibility, subdivision and building permissions must be checked locally.

CONCEPT REVISION RECOMMENDED

Test 20 villas versus 24 villas

Compare net realisation, view quality, road and utility costs, regulatory feasibility, density, outdoor space and staged absorption. Do not freeze the masterplan on survey preference alone.

07 / Next validation steps

  1. Collect legally compliant, consented responses from genuine target purchasers.
  2. Obtain measured topography, tree inventory, access survey and view analysis.
  3. Benchmark comparable sale transactions, not just asking prices.
  4. Run preference trade-off and statistically designed price research.
  5. Prepare alternative layouts and a costed project feasibility model.
  6. Validate all land, building, transfer and buyer-eligibility rules.
